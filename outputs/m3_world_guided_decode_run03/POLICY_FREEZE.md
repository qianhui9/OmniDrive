# Final-policy boundary

Before heldout inference, the single candidate policy was frozen: complete exact K8 history, <=4 raw/conditioned candidates from <=2 previous records, processed prefill first-token scoring, causal EWMA acceptance and block/single CUDA cost estimates, and suppression after two unproductive verifications. No online native-answer or annotation access. The final run checks all measured source bytes at completion.

The development test uses the old v2 three scenes and one fixed-three-QA B/C round (24 full requests). Both accepted389 tokens, executed383 target forwards and emitted731 output tokens. Its source files are preserved byte-for-byte in development_fixed/executed_sources. Its 1.0065x B/C E2E ratio is a single development round, not final independent evidence.

Finalization after this test only calibrated historical cost statistics using measured single/block CUDA events, and classified “clear path” as a full-scene safety dependency instead of positive vehicle existence. No additional answer templates or K search were added. Final-policy offline overlap on these old scenes has only one >=2-token conditioned candidate among36 natural QA, three generic introductory tokens without reaching class content. This weak upper bound limits expected natural-stream benefit; it does not stop the required finite heldout evaluation.

The three final scene IDs and their logs in experiment_plan.json exclude v1/v2 scenes/logs and M1 qualification calibration/validation logs. No heldout current answer or annotation was inspected to alter the online policy. Future heldout diagnostics/quality are offline reporting only. No further optimization iteration is authorized by positive/negative heldout results in this run.

An initial v3 primary attempt found a road-type/ego-vehicle task-scope bug after320 complete requests. It was interrupted, archived and excluded, with exact source bytes. The fix assigns explicit road-type questions to road dependencies and negated questions to full-scene incomplete dependencies. No new answer templates were added. The final cohort excludes these three additional scenes AND logs, as recorded in excluded_scope_debug_scene_ids. This corrected policy is frozen before replacement heldout inference.
